Fingerprinting Test Lab

This page runs the same tests that tracking websites use to recognize your browser without cookies. Compare what they see before and after you turn on PrintShield.

📋 Try it in three steps

  1. Task 1 (See): The tests below ran when this page opened. Click the PrintShield icon in your toolbar to see which details this page checked.
  2. Task 2 (Learn): In the popup, click "Learn more" on any item to read what it reveals and why it can identify you.
  3. Task 3 (Mitigate & Compare): Click Turn on protection in the popup, then reload this page. Your Fingerprint ID now changes on every reload, so trackers can no longer recognize you, and your hardware details become common ones.
