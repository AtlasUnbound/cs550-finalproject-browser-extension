📊 Reading your amiunique.org / Cover Your Tracks results
  • "Unique" canvas, WebGL data and audio values are expected. With protection on they change on every visit, so they can never be matched again. Cover Your Tracks labels them "randomized".
  • amiunique.org compares you with years of past visitors. Anything tied to a brand-new browser version, such as the user agent, the navigator property list and WebGL extensions, looks rare there until more people update. Cover Your Tracks only uses recent visitors.
  • The two sites disagree on some values. A UTC time zone, for example, is common on amiunique.org but rare on Cover Your Tracks. PrintShield aims for values that are common in real browsers today.
  • Open the test from the address bar, not from a Google result, and don't reload it: reloads and revisits add cache headers (If-None-Match, Cache-Control) that no extension can remove.
  • Reload the test page after changing settings, because some protections apply only to pages loaded afterwards. Headers like "Cache-Control" appear only because you reloaded.
